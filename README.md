Infinite Horizons

This modpack is a curated Minecraft experience built for survival, progression, and discovery. It mixes magic, technology, automation, and exploration into one world that feels both rewarding and fresh from the start.

You will find a wide range of mods that support building, farming, crafting, storage, travel, and adventure. The pack is designed to guide you through the early game while still leaving plenty of room for your own ideas and goals.

The world is tuned for long play sessions and steady growth. Quests help introduce important systems, but the real fun comes from experimenting, expanding your base, and learning how each part of the pack fits together.

This pack runs on Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.4.1. A strong machine and at least 10 GB of RAM are recommended for the best experience.

If you are new to modded Minecraft, start with the quests, take things one step at a time, and enjoy the journey. This pack rewards patience, curiosity, and a willingness to explore.
